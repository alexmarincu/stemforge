# yt-dlp and yt-dlp-ejs

- Version: 2026.10.26, 0.8.0
- Licence: Unlicense AND MIT AND ISC
- Homepage: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
- Source: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/tag/2026.10.26

`yt-dlp` (Unlicense) performs the YouTube download and `yt-dlp-ejs` (Unlicense
AND MIT AND ISC) provides the JavaScript challenge solver, which runs on the
bundled Deno. Both ship as Python wheels inside the frozen payload; neither
contacts a StemForge server and neither is updated at runtime.
