Rubber Band 4.0.0 is built from tag `v4.0.0` (commit
`1d95888bec3ae0a17c0c4af791810d5a63f6bc35`) with builtin FFT and resampler.
Only the command-line tool is shipped; it statically contains `librubberband`
and dynamically links the co-located `libsndfile`. The Meson configure line is
recorded in `LICENSES/rubberband/BUILDINFO.txt`. Used under the "or later"
option of GPL-2.0, which is compatible with StemForge's GPL-3.0-only licence.
