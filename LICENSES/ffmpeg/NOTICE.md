FFmpeg and ffprobe are built by [BtbN FFmpeg Builds](https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds)
as the GPL static variant, release `autobuild-2026-11-15-12-40`, version
`n9.0.2-57-gd838f9f9ae`. The shipped `ffmpeg -buildconf` output is captured by
the release build into `LICENSES/ffmpeg/BUILDINFO.txt`.

The GPL build statically includes, among others, `libmp3lame` (LGPL-2.1),
`librubberband` (GPL-2.0-or-later), `libx264` and `libx265` (GPL-2.0-or-later),
`libsoxr` (LGPL-2.1), `libopus` and `libvorbis` (BSD-3-Clause), zlib, and
OpenSSL (Apache-2.0). The exact list and commits are recorded in
`LICENSES/ffmpeg/BUILDINFO.txt` and the release's third-party source bundle.
`ffplay`, the documentation, and the presets are not shipped.
