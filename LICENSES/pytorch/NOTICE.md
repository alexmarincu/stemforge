PyTorch 2.15.0+cpu is the CPU-only wheel. Its `dist-info/licenses/` tree
(BSD-3-Clause for PyTorch plus oneDNN, fbgemm, LLVM OpenMP, sleef, protobuf and
other third-party notices) is pooled verbatim under `LICENSES/texts/` and
indexed in `LICENSES/python/DEPENDENCIES.md`. The Linux wheel also conveys GCC
runtime libraries and the Windows wheel conveys `libiomp5md.dll`; both are
noticed separately below.
