PyInstaller 6.22.3 is a build-time tool; only its bootloader is embedded in the
shipped executable. The bootloader is GPL-2.0-or-later with the PyInstaller
Bootloader Exception, which permits unlimited linking and distribution of the
resulting executable. PyInstaller's run-time hooks and fake modules are
Apache-2.0.
